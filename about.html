SleepWell was founded in 2009 by Dr. Amara Singh and Dr. James Okonkwo — two fellowship-trained somnologists who shared a vision: make world-class sleep medicine accessible, compassionate, and evidence-driven for every patient who walks through our doors.

Our mission is simple: Better Sleep. Better Health. We combine board-certified clinical expertise with the latest diagnostic technology to diagnose, treat, and educate — transforming our patients' quality of life.

Why SleepWell

The SleepWell Difference

We set the standard for patient-centred sleep medicine with clinical rigour and genuine care.

Gold-Standard Diagnostics

Full in-lab polysomnography with 32-channel EEG, SpO₂ monitoring, end-tidal CO₂, and video recording — interpreted same-day by a board-certified sleep physician.

Multidisciplinary Team

Somnologists, pulmonologists, ENT surgeons, dental sleep medicine specialists, and licensed behavioral therapists collaborate on every complex case.

Patient-Centred Care

From first consultation through long-term CPAP adherence support, our care coordinators guide you every step of the way with personalised follow-up plans.

Cutting-Edge Technology

Natus Grael 4K amplifiers, WatchPAT One home sleep testing, ResMed AirSense 11 AutoCPAP, and AI-assisted scoring software for the most accurate results.

Insurance & Compliance

We are in-network with major PPO plans and Medicare. Our billing team handles prior authorisations so you can focus on your health, not paperwork.

Research & Education

Active IRB-approved clinical trials, residency teaching affiliation, and a public sleep-education library empowering patients and the wider community.
