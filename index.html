Targeted Clinical Treatments

Recognizing Common Sleep Disorders

Untreated sleep disorders elevate cardiovascular risk, blood pressure, and mental exhaustion. Our clinical team identifies and treats the root cause.

Obstructive Sleep Apnea

Repeated collapse of upper airway tissue causing loud snoring, oxygen desaturations, and micro-awakenings throughout the night.

Treatment: Auto-CPAP & Oral Devices

Chronic Insomnia

Persistent difficulty initiating or maintaining sleep, nighttime hyperarousal, racing thoughts, and unrefreshing morning waking.

Treatment: Evidence-Based CBT-I

Restless Legs & PLMD

Irresistible urge to move the lower limbs in evening hours, accompanied by periodic limb twitching that fragments deep sleep.

Treatment: Neurological Therapy

CPAP Mask Intolerance

Struggling with mask air leaks, bridge-of-nose ulcers, claustrophobia, or dry nasal passages from inappropriate pressure levels.

Treatment: 3D Mask Refitting & EPR
